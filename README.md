## About Me

I recently completed my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science and am currently seeking entry-level opportunities in AI/ML development, GenAI applications, and data-driven engineering roles.

Over the course of my final year, I built and shipped several end-to-end AI systems on my own — spanning cybersecurity automation, predictive maintenance, retrieval-augmented document intelligence, and multilingual conversational AI. My focus is on turning practical problems into working systems, not just prototypes.

I enjoy working close to the full stack of an AI application: designing the pipeline, integrating models, building the interface, and deploying it so it actually runs.
